Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026

In short: DeepSopor works without an account, and all of your sounds, mixes and settings stay on your device. To manage subscriptions and measure our marketing, the App uses two third-party services (Adapty and Singular) that process limited technical identifiers and purchase information. We do not collect your name, email, contacts or location.

This Privacy Policy describes how DeepSopor ("the App", "we", "us") handles information when you use our mobile application on Android and iOS. By using the App, you agree to the practices described here.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

The App does not require registration and does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, or location. We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use your information for profiling or for showing advertisements inside the App.

2. Information Collected Through Third-Party Services

The App integrates two third-party SDKs. They collect limited information automatically when you use the App:

ServicePurposeData processed
Adapty Subscription and purchase management (verifying and unlocking Premium) Purchase and subscription status, transaction identifiers, app-generated user ID, device identifiers, app version, OS version, in-app interactions with the paywall
Singular Advertising attribution (measuring which marketing campaigns lead to installs and purchases) Advertising identifier (Google advertising ID on Android; IDFA on iOS, only if you allow tracking), device identifiers, IP address, install and app-open events, purchase events, device and app information

These providers act as service providers on our behalf and process the data only to deliver their services to us. Their handling of data is also governed by their own privacy policies:

  • Adapty: adapty.io/privacy-policy
  • Singular: singular.net/privacy-policy

3. How We Use Information

  • To process, verify and restore your subscription or purchase.
  • To provide and maintain the features you paid for.
  • To measure the performance of our advertising and understand how users find the App.
  • To prevent fraud and keep the App secure.

Legal bases (EEA and UK users). We process subscription and purchase data because it is necessary to perform our contract with you (providing the features you paid for). We process attribution and technical data based on our legitimate interest in measuring and improving our marketing, balanced against your rights and expectations. Where local law requires consent for use of the advertising ID, we rely on your device-level settings (on iOS, the App Tracking Transparency prompt), which you can change at any time (see Section 7).

4. Data Stored on Your Device

Your settings, preferences, saved sound mixes, and timer configuration are stored locally on your device only. All audio content is bundled inside the App, and this data never leaves your device. If you uninstall the App, this data is permanently deleted.

5. In-App Purchases

If you choose to purchase a subscription or a one-time upgrade, the payment is processed entirely by Google Play Billing (Android) or the Apple App Store (iOS). We never see, receive, or store your credit card number, bank details, or other payment credentials. Google or Apple provides limited transaction information (such as the purchase status of your entitlement), which is verified through Adapty so that the App can unlock the features you paid for.

You can view, manage, or cancel your subscription at any time in the Google Play Store (Android) or in your Apple ID subscription settings (iOS).

6. Information Sharing

We share information only in the following cases:

  • Service providers — Adapty and Singular, as described above.
  • Google and Apple — in connection with Google Play and App Store purchases and services.
  • Legal reasons — if required by law, or to protect our rights, users, or the security of the App.

We do not sell your personal information to data brokers, and we do not use it to build advertising profiles for targeted advertising in other apps. Sharing with Singular is limited to measuring the performance of our own marketing campaigns.

7. Advertising ID and Your Choices

Your device's advertising ID is a resettable identifier.

  • Android: you can reset or delete it, or opt out of ads personalization, in Settings > Privacy (or Google) > Ads.
  • iOS: the App asks for your permission before tracking you across other companies' apps and websites (App Tracking Transparency). If you decline, the IDFA is not accessed. You can change this at any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.

If you delete or do not allow the advertising ID, attribution through that identifier will no longer be possible. You can also contact us to request that your data be removed from our attribution records (see Section 10).

8. Permissions

  • Network access — used for purchase verification, subscription management, attribution, and app updates.
  • Background audio (iOS) / Foreground service & notification (Android) — used solely to keep audio playing reliably while your screen is off. Any notification contains no personal data.
  • Audio settings — used to manage sound focus so playback does not conflict with other apps.
  • Advertising ID / Tracking permission — the attribution SDK reads the advertising ID only as described in Section 2 (on iOS, only if you allow tracking).

9. Data Retention and Security

Data held by our service providers is kept only as long as necessary for the purposes above, or as required by law. Local data on your device is deleted when you uninstall the App. We do not operate our own user database, and data in transit to our providers is encrypted using HTTPS. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we work to protect the information that is processed.

10. Your Rights and Data Requests

Depending on where you live (for example, in the EEA, UK, or California), you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or restrict the use of your personal data, and to object to certain processing. Because the App has no accounts, we may ask for your device or advertising ID to locate your data. To make a request, email us at deepsopor@yeah.net. We will respond within a reasonable time, as required by applicable law.

11. International Data Transfers

Our service providers may process data in countries other than your own. Where required, they rely on appropriate safeguards for such transfers.

12. Children's Privacy

The App is intended for users aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. We do not verify users' ages, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child under 13 has used the App, please contact us and we will take steps to delete any related information we can identify.

Because the App offers paid subscriptions, we encourage parents and guardians to supervise purchases, for example by turning on purchase approval in Google Play parental controls (Android) or "Ask to Buy" in Apple Family Sharing (iOS).

13. Changes to This Policy

If we update this Privacy Policy, we will change the "Last updated" date above and publish the revised version on this page.

14. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at deepsopor@yeah.net. Our Terms of Service are also available.
