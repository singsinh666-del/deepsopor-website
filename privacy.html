Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how DeepSopor ("the App", "we", "us") handles information when you use our Android application. We designed the App so that it works without an account and without collecting personal data about you.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

The App does not require registration and does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, or location. We do not include advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, or third-party trackers in the App. We do not sell or share your personal information with anyone.

2. Data Stored on Your Device

Your settings, preferences, saved sound mixes, and timer configuration are stored locally on your device only. This data never leaves your device. If you uninstall the App, this data is permanently deleted.

3. In-App Purchases

If you choose to purchase a subscription or a one-time upgrade, the payment is processed entirely by Google Play Billing. We never see, receive, or store your credit card number, bank details, or other payment credentials. Google may provide us with limited transaction-related information (such as the purchase status of your entitlement) so that the App can unlock the features you paid for. If we use a subscription-management service provider to verify purchases, that provider acts as a processor on our behalf and is bound by its own privacy policy.

You can view, manage, or cancel your subscription at any time in the Google Play Store.

4. Permissions

  • Network access — used for purchase verification with Google Play and for app updates.
  • Foreground service & notification — used solely to keep audio playing reliably while your screen is off. The notification contains no personal data.
  • Audio settings — used to manage sound focus so playback does not conflict with other apps.

5. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Data Security

Because the App does not operate its own servers and does not collect personal data, there is no central database of user information that could be breached. Data that exists stays under the control of the operating system on your device.

7. Changes to This Policy

If we update this Privacy Policy, we will change the "Last updated" date above and publish the revised version on this page.

8. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at deepsopor@yeah.net.
